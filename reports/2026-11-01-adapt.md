# Responsive adaptation — 2026-11-01

Released in Pencils 0.20.127 on 2026-11-01, with the narrow desktop follow-up in 0.20.128 and verification follow-up 0.20.129 on 2026-11-02. Impeccable adapt pass on phones, iPad-sized touch windows and desktop. The existing compact interface and shared navigation remain the design authority.

## Findings and changes

- At 320px, the card editor's seven header actions overlapped: the management group crowded Close. The smallest panels now put management actions on their own row, retaining direct access to every action and 44px touch targets.
- At 640px, the card's field box was 205px wide but its contents needed 246px; priority spilled into the dates. Landscape phones also had a small overflow. Compact touch field boxes now show the person's configured short name, while the accessible select and tooltip retain their full name. Wider boxes retain longer names, truncated only when necessary.
- At 320px, calendar buttons left too little room for complete dates. The same range box now stacks Start and Due where they cannot fit side by side, returning to one row when the panel widens.
- An additional mouse-driven check at 620–640px found the Status column's fixed minimum also overflowing its field box. Removing that minimum lets Status use the available width, while preserving the longer assignee label and the priority button. The regression failed before this follow-up and the rendered 620px, 640px and 700px checks now show no overflow.

These are container-based adaptations: they follow the space available to the editor rather than adding a separate phone or tablet implementation. No dependency, data format, navigation action or custom gesture was added.

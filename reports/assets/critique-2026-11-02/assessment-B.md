## Confirmed issues to consider in synthesis

### B1 — P1: Checklist touch targets are 13×13px; nearby taps enter the editor

`public/document-editor.js:105–137` constructs a bare `.document-checkbox` input. `public/project-documents.css:349` only vertically aligns it. On phone and iPad it measures **13×13px**, no associated label, no enlarged pseudo hit target, within a 28px line. A real touch **12px to the right of its centre** (well inside a nominal 44px target) hits `.cm-line` and focuses the contenteditable `.cm-content`; the checkbox remains unticked. This is a concrete route to the keyboard appearing when a user tries to tick a task. The ordinary exact-centre tap handler does prevent editor focus, so preserve it while enlarging the target.

Evidence: `probe.json` entries `phone/documents` and `ipad/documents`; screenshots `phone-checkbox-near-tap.png`, `ipad-checkbox-near-tap.png`. Existing `e2e/documents/document-checkbox-touch.spec.mjs` tests centre taps only. Fix the shared checkbox widget/hit area with 44px touch targets and an off-centre tap regression; retain desktop compactness and scroll gestures. These are not the phone task-row title false positives: the title's existing `::after` was probed and does provide a full-height target.

### B2 — P2: Light-mode card-search hint is too faint

`public/styles.css:630–632` hard-codes `.search input::placeholder { color: #9ba290; }`. The computed placeholder is `rgb(155,162,144)` at opacity 1 over `#f9f9f5` on phone/desktop (**2.5:1**), or `#fdfdf9` in iPad's top bar (**2.6:1**). Dark mode is separately overridden and did not trigger. This survives direct computed-style checking; axe's clean result did not catch it. Reuse the shared placeholder/muted token and assert the rendered placeholder contrast on the actual backgrounds, leaving input dimensions unchanged. Evidence: all three light List overlay samples and `probe.json`.

### B3 — Withdrawn after adjudication: frame control follows intentional board geometry

The original measurement (37.4×37.4px at 85% zoom) is accurate, but treating it as a defect was incorrect. `e2e/board/ipad-board-zoom.spec.mjs:1–5,120–140` and `public/controls.css:250–264` establish the distinction: world controls retain their document/board-space dimensions, while unscaled tools and menus retain 44px targets. Inverse sizing world controls has previously crowded card titles and changed geometry while zooming. Do not introduce it from this critique.

A fresh disposable fixture verified the existing alternative at `public/canvas-pro.js:1698`: open Frames (phone: Navigate → Frames; iPad: Frames tool), then use the frame row's collapse/expand action. At **85% and 50% zoom on both phone and iPad**, the Frames opener and row action were **44×44px**, outside `#canvas-world`. Actual touch taps collapsed and re-expanded the frame; both changes were confirmed persisted in the temporary store. All four flows had zero console/page errors. The small world button remains an intentional compact shortcut with a full-size alternative.

Adjudication evidence: `/tmp/pencils-critique-B/frame-adjudication.json`, reproduction `frame-adjudication.mjs`, screenshots `phone-frame-outline-0.85.png`, `phone-frame-outline-0.5.png`, `ipad-frame-outline-0.85.png`, `ipad-frame-outline-0.5.png`. The temporary server/browser/vault were closed in `finally`; no overlay server was started. This conclusion supersedes the initial B3 priority and inverse-zoom suggestion.

### B4 — P2: Home leaks stored status and date values instead of the shared display labels

`public/home.js:146` renders `esc(t.status)` and `esc(t.due)` directly in Card list panels. The seeded Home shows **`in-progress`, `todo`, `2026-11-03`** while the same cards in List show **`In progress`, `To do`, `3 Nov`**. This weakens the single-record / consistent-view promise and custom status names can expose internal identifiers. Use the existing status label and local date formatting helpers here. Evidence: `desktop-light-home.png`, `ipad-light-list.png` and source at line 146. Keep the established compact panel layout.

### B5 — P3: Home save status remains 10px on iPad

Home reuses `.board-save` for `#home-save`, but its status reads at 10px even on iPad. The 10px avatar exception is purposeful; saving reassurance is not an avatar. Reuse an existing secondary text token for this status (and reconcile the shared rule rather than make a special larger Home bar). This can improve readability without spending more vertical space. Evidence: four Home overlay findings, `public/home.js:76` and `.board-save` styling.

### Additional source-only copy issue

`public/index.html:9` describes Pencils as “An offline, Markdown-powered project workspace.” PRODUCT.md explicitly says the main workspace needs a reachable server, with separate offline drawing/checkpoint capabilities. The metadata overstates the general offline workflow. Replace the unqualified “offline” claim with an accurate description; no new product claim is needed. This is source-only evidence, not a tested network-loss failure.

## Boundaries and conclusions

The compact design itself is not a detector defect. The sampled toolbar controls and common actions generally honor their full touch targets, light/dark layout remains coherent, and no normal-page overflow or application errors appeared. Confirmed low-contrast search and tiny checklist targets deserve attention; zoom-scaled world controls are intentional and have a verified full-size Frames alternative, and the three static detector findings do not warrant changes.

No physical-device keyboard, pen calibration, WebKit/Firefox, Compact density, large-board scale, slow network, mutation/recovery, all-menu interaction or release tests were run in this assessment. The near-checkbox tap demonstrates focus in Chromium emulation, not a filmed physical iOS keyboard. This was evidence gathering only; no fixes were made.
